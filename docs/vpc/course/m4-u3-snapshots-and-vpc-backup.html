Snapshots and VPC Backup

By the end of this unit you'll know how to take a consistent snapshot of two volumes, copy it to another region, restore from it, and tell how a backup policy differs from a snapshot you take by hand.

About 10 minutesNo account needed5 steps

Northgate Freight is a made-up haulier. Before a change, it wants a copy of an app server's two disks that agree with each other, and later it wants a copy every night. The names and output are real, even though the company is made up.

Every command and line of output here comes from real runs. IDs are shortened.

Step 1 · Explore the parts

From flush to restore

There are four stages: flush, snapshot the two volumes together, copy to another region, and restore. Select a part of the drawing to see what it does and which lines make it, or click a line. We left out the tag options.

↑ tap any box

Flush

IBM says to make sure cached data is on disk before a snapshot. sync in the guest does that.

Watch for this. A snapshot is crash-consistent. It holds what was on disk, not what the guest held in memory.

Consistency group

Snapshots of several volumes of one server, taken together so that they agree. A snapshot is a point-in-time copy of one volume, kept in a regional storage repository. The first copies the whole volume; later ones copy only changes. A group pauses I/O for a short time, from a few milliseconds up to 4 seconds.

From vSphere: the nearest word is Snapshot. A VPC snapshot is of one volume, and it doesn't cover the whole VM. This is a rough mapping.

Watch for this. A group can't include snapshots of mixed storage generations.

Copy to another region

You make the copy from the target region, with the source snapshot's CRN. Only one copy can exist in each region. The time grows with the volume's size, and IBM's example is up to 12.5 hours for 3 TB.

Watch for this. You can't copy a consistency group to another zone or region, so copy its member snapshots one by one.

Restore

Restoring creates a new, fully provisioned volume and doesn't overwrite the parent. Data starts to copy at once, and performance is reduced until all of it has arrived.

Watch for this. IBM doesn't support restoring a server directly from a consistency group, so restore its volumes separately.

sync
BOOT=$(ibmcloud is instance vpct-prod-app-z1-01 --output json | jq -r '.boot_volume_attachment.volume.id')
DATA=$(ibmcloud is volume vpct-prod-app-z1-01-data1 --output json | jq -r .id)
cat > cg.json <<JSON
[{"name": "vpct-prod-app-z1-01-boot-snap", "source_volume": {"id": "$BOOT"}},
 {"name": "vpct-prod-app-z1-01-data1-snap", "source_volume": {"id": "$DATA"}}]
JSON
ibmcloud is snapshot-consistency-group-create --snapshots @cg.json --name vpct-prod-app-z1-01-cg --resource-group-name vpct-lab
SRC=$(ibmcloud is snapshot vpct-prod-app-z1-01-data1-snap --output json | jq -r .crn)
ibmcloud target -r us-south -g vpct-lab
ibmcloud is snapshot-create --source-snapshot-crn $SRC --name vpct-dr-app-z1-01-data1-snap --resource-group-name vpct-lab
ibmcloud is volume-create vpct-dr-app-z1-01-data1 general-purpose us-south-1 --snapshot vpct-dr-app-z1-01-data1-snap --resource-group-name vpct-lab

Step 3 · Run it, on a schedule

A backup policy makes the same snapshots

Backup for VPC is IBM Cloud's service that makes snapshots on a schedule and deletes them after a retention period. A policy holds target tags and up to four plans. We cut output with ....

bash

# MM HH is a time a few minutes ahead

ibmcloud is backup-policy-create --name vpct-prod-daily-bp \
  --match-tags backup:vpct-daily --match-resource-type instance \
  --included-content boot_volume,data_volumes \
  --plan-name vpct-prod-daily-plan --plan-cron-spec "MM HH * * *" --plan-active \
  --plan-delete-after 1 \
  --resource-group-name vpct-lab

Step 4 · Spot the fault

An error code and a tag in the wrong place

Northgate tries to make one consistency group from a data volume on the sdp profile and a first-generation boot volume. The command returns this error:

snapshot_consistency_groups_different_profile_not_supported

What caused it?

Show the answer

The members are of different storage generations. A test run tried exactly this and got this error. A group of one sdp volume alone was created. The rule is that a group can't include snapshots of mixed storage generations.

Northgate makes a policy with --match-resource-type instance. It then puts the tag backup:vpct-daily on the two volumes instead of on the server.

Where does IBM say the tag goes?

Show the answer

On the server. Matching by instance backs up a server's volumes as a consistency group, and for that IBM says the tag goes on the server. The lab tagged the server.

Step 5 · Check yourself

Four questions

A snapshot is crash-consistent. What does that mean?

Show the answer

It holds what was on disk at that moment. That's why IBM says to flush cached data to disk first. Application-consistent backups are done by Backup and Recovery, through an agent. Unit 4.4 covers that.

You want a copy of a consistency group in us-south. What do you do?

Show the answer

Copy its member snapshots one by one. A consistency group can't be copied to another zone or region, and only one copy of a snapshot can exist in each region.

Does a restore overwrite the volume the snapshot came from?

Show the answer

No, it creates a new volume. Both runs restored to a new volume and left the source untouched. IBM also says backups and snapshots aren't a disaster recovery solution, because restoring from either is a manual operation that takes time.

Review from unit 4.2. A volume's Encryption line reads provider_managed. What does that tell you?

Show the answer

It has no key of yours, and IBM holds the keys. A volume without a key of yours shows provider_managed. A volume with one shows user_managed. IBM-managed encryption is the default.
