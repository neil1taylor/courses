Revoke on a load balancer, and the mistakes

By the end of this unit you'll be able to give a load balancer listener a revocation list that works, predict what an update does to open connections, and match a symptom to the set-up mistake behind it.

About 10 minutesNo account needed5 steps

Northgate Freight is a made-up haulier. Its web application sits behind a load balancer that asks each client for a certificate, and the security team wants a revoked certificate refused there too.

Every command and line of output in this unit comes from real runs.

Step 1 · Explore the parts

A listener that checks client certificates

A load balancer listener can check a client certificate with mutual TLS. Select a part to see what it needs, and what the runs showed.

↑ tap any box

Listener

The load balancer does not read the CRL distribution point. With a Secrets Manager CA and no list on the listener, the revoked certificate was accepted for the whole time it was checked, 2 hours.

Watch for this. IBM's page does not say whether a load balancer reads the distribution point of a certificate. IBM says the CRL is optional PEM text, and the load balancer applies a new CRL immediately for subsequent connections.

Client CA secret

The secret that --client-auth-ca-crn names holds the CA certificate and nothing else: no intermediate certificate and no private key. Use a CA of one tier, a root that signs the client certificates.

Watch for this. The listener refused a private certificate secret as its client CA, with listener_invalid_certificate.

Revocation list

Uploaded to the listener with --client-auth-crl. A listener that held the secret and no list accepted both certificates. After the list was added by an update, the revoked certificate was refused at the next check, 11 seconds after the update command.

Watch for this. An update that gave only --client-auth-ca-crn kept the list that the listener already held.

Update the list

New connections with the revoked certificate were refused 19 to 24 seconds after the update command. The listener stayed update_pending for about 80 seconds.

Watch for this. Every open connection on the listener was cut by an update, and a connection with a good certificate was cut as well. A client of this listener has to reconnect after each update.

Two-tier CA

With a root and an intermediate that signs the clients, a list on the listener refused every client, the good ones too, with unknown ca. A file with both CAs' lists in it was refused with listener_invalid_crl_format.

Watch for this. So for a load balancer the path that worked was a one-tier CA.

ibmcloud secrets-manager secret-create --secret-name vpct-own-alb-client-ca --secret-type imported_cert --imported-cert-certificate "$(cat ca.crt)"
ibmcloud is load-balancer-listener-create vpct-prod-web-alb --protocol https --port 8445 --default-pool $POOL --certificate-instance-crn $CERT_CRN --client-auth-ca-crn <client-ca-crn> --client-auth-crl @crl.pem --vpc vpct-prod
ibmcloud is load-balancer-listener-update vpct-prod-web-alb <listener-id> --client-auth-crl @crl-new.pem --vpc vpct-prod
Step 2 · Choose it

Set up the listener

Pick what Northgate does, then read the worked answer.

Which CA design should Northgate use for the client certificates of a load balancer listener?

Show the worked answer

One tier. With a list on the listener, a two-tier CA refused every client, and the listener refused a private certificate secret as its client CA.

A laptop's certificate is revoked. What does Northgate do on the listener?

Show the worked answer

Make a new list and update the listener with it. The listener does not read the distribution point. Every open connection is cut by the update, so clients reconnect.

The listener now holds a list that names the revoked certificate. What does curl print with the revoked certificate?

Show the answer

A TLS alert that the certificate is revoked. The good certificate gets the page.

curl -k --cert <chain>.pem --key <key>.pem https://<alb-host-name>:8445/
vpct-backend
curl: (56) OpenSSL SSL_read: OpenSSL/3.0.13: error:0A000414:SSL routines::sslv3 alert certificate revoked, errno 0

The first line is the good certificate. The second is the revoked one.

The alert tells the cause. Which cause gives sslv3 alert certificate expired?

Show the answer

The list is past its next-update date. A certificate in the list gives certificate revoked. A list that cannot be used with the chain gives unknown ca. No client certificate gives tlsv13 alert certificate required.

Step 4 · Spot the fault

Which set-up mistake is it?

Each set-up below let a revoked certificate in, and none gave an error. Match the symptom to the mistake.

On a VPN server, the revoked certificate connected after 75 minutes and after 120 minutes, on a new server and after a list rotation. No error.

Show the answer

An imported CA secret as the client CA. The VPN client CA must be a Secrets Manager private certificate secret.

The VPN server took the CA without an error, and the revoked certificate connected 37 minutes after it was revoked.

Show the answer

A CA without CRL distribution points. It writes no list address into its certificates, so nothing can refuse them.

The VPN server accepted a list from the root, but the intermediate signed the client. The revoked certificate still connected.

Show the answer

A list from the wrong CA in the chain. The certificate was refused 58 seconds after the intermediate's list was applied.

Step 5 · Check yourself

Three questions

You update a listener's list. What happens to open connections?

Show the answer

Every open connection on the listener was cut, and a connection with a good certificate was cut as well. It ended 23 or 24 seconds after the command. A client has to reconnect after each update.

A listener has a Secrets Manager CA and no list. Why was a revoked certificate accepted?

Show the answer

The load balancer does not read the CRL distribution point. Upload the list to the listener with --client-auth-crl.

A client sends no certificate to the listener. What does curl print?

Show the answer

tlsv13 alert certificate required. The other two mean the certificate is in the list, or the list cannot be used with the chain.

You can revoke on a load balancer

Your check score–Answer the three questions in step 5.
Remember

A load balancer listener does not read the distribution point, so you upload the list, with a one-tier CA. Each update cuts every open connection. A revoked certificate gets in through an imported VPN client CA, a CA without distribution points, a list from the wrong CA, or no list. A list past its next-update date refuses everyone.
