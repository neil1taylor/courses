Unit 3 of Module 1

What it costs and where the data sits

By the end you can say how BRS charges for backup storage, which costs sit outside that price, and where IBM says the backups are kept and who can reach them.

About 10 minutesNo account needed5 steps

Northgate Freight is a made-up company. Its servers are in Frankfurt and its finance team wants a cost range and its security team wants to know where the copies live.

Nothing in this unit was run by hand. The prices are IBM's list prices in USD for customers billed in the USA, read from IBM's public catalog. IBM's prices change, so a price here is a reading, not a quote.

Step 1 · Predict

How much for a terabyte?

BRS has one plan, Premium, billed per GB-hour of backup storage, and the price scales linearly with backup size. The price depends on the region.

Northgate keeps about 1 TB (1,024 GB) of backups in eu-de for a month. What does the backup storage cost at list price?

Show the answerAbout USD 113 per TB-month.

That is IBM's list price for eu-de (and eu-es). A TB here is 1,024 GB.

Which region is the cheapest for backup storage, at those prices?

Show the answer

us-south and us-east, at 0.000130 USD per GB-hour. br-sao is the dearest, at 0.000167. The prices are IBM's list prices.

Which GB does BRS bill: the logical size of the data, the data read, or the data written after dedup and compression?

Show the answer

IBM's FAQ defines all three terms but does not link any of them to billing, and the catalog does not say. A cost estimate should state that gap.

Step 2 · Explore the parts

What is in the price and what is not

IBM's catalog prices only the storage. The other costs come from the parts you run yourself, at normal IBM Cloud prices. Select a part of the drawing to see what it is.

↑ tap any box

Backup storage

The one plan is Premium. It is paid only and cannot be changed after the instance is created. IBM says the price "will scale linearly depending on backup size". It is charged per GB-hour, at a price that depends on the region.

Watch for this. IBM does not say which GB is billed: the logical size, the data read, or the data written after dedup and compression.

Backup copy

A separate price line for a second copy of backups: USD 0.000137 per GB-hour in every region. What it covers is not documented.

Connector servers

You pay for them at normal IBM Cloud prices: at least 2, each with 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM and a 171 GB disk.

Network parts

VPE gateways, floating IPs, Transit Gateway and data transfer, where you use them.

Cluster capacity

For a Kubernetes or OpenShift cluster: the worker capacity used by the connector, Velero and Datamover.

Keys and logs

Key Protect or HPCS, if you use them. Activity Tracker, Cloud Logs and Monitoring.

Step 3 · Predict

Where the copies sit

Northgate's security team asks where the backups will live. IBM's architecture pages give an answer for some of it and are silent on the rest.

Northgate's servers are in eu-de. In which region should it create the instance?

Show the answer

IBM advises putting the instance in the same region as the workloads. A connector built in a VPC must be created in the same location as the instance. In the console you set Location when you order.

Where does IBM say the backups are stored?

Show the answer

IBM stores backups in "regionally durable storage", in the zones of the instance's region. IBM's encryption pages describe the store as IBM Cloud Object Storage buckets, encrypted with keys. The customer never creates a bucket.

Does BRS keep a copy of Northgate's backups in a second region?

Show the answer

No cross-region copy is documented. The pricing lists a "backup copy" metric, which suggests one exists or is planned, but what it covers is not documented. The documents also describe no failover of the service or the backups if a whole region is lost.

Step 4 · Spot the fault

Three claims about the data

Northgate's security review lists three statements. Find the ones the documents do not support.

A colleague writes: "The backups sit in an Object Storage bucket in our own account, and we set its retention." What do IBM's documents say?

Show the answer

IBM says it is responsible for tenants, endpoints, networking and storage. Which account owns the buckets is not stated. IBM's documents do not settle it, though the key authorization from the service to your Key Protect fits a store that IBM owns.

Northgate wants its own encryption key. When must it be chosen?

Show the answer

The key is set when the instance is created, by giving the root key. The IBM Cloud authorization must exist first: from Backup and Recovery Service to Key Protect, role Reader. IBM offers Key Protect (bring your own key) and Hyper Protect Crypto Services (keep your own key). Changing the key later is not documented.

Can Northgate switch off the public endpoint of its instance, so that only the private one answers?

Show the answer

Public and private endpoints are both on by default and neither can be turned off. Context-based restrictions are the way to limit access. The dashboard must be reached over the public network.

Step 5 · Check yourself

Three questions

How does BRS charge for backup storage?

Show the answer

The catalog shows a per-GB-hour metric and no instance fee or per-source fee. IBM's plan text says the price scales linearly with backup size.

Review from unit 1.1. Who deploys the connectors?

Show the answer

You deploy and scale connectors and agents. IBM runs the back end.

Northgate runs two connector servers. Where does that cost appear?

Show the answer

Connector VSIs are listed among the costs that are not in the plan.
