Unit 3 of Module 1

What it is not: logical isolation, no physical air gap, and the Beta's terms

By the end you can say what "isolated" means for this vault, say that it is not a physical air gap, and list the terms of the Beta that Northgate must accept.

About 10 minutesNo account needed5 steps

Northgate Freight is a made-up haulier. Its board has heard the words "air gap". Before Northgate repeats them, it needs to know exactly what it would be promising.

The screen comes from the IBM Cloud console in our lab on 10 November 2026. The results come from tests on 5 and 6 October 2026. The vault is IBM Cloud Cyber Vault (Beta), in us-south, with no SLA.

Step 1 · Explore the parts

What keeps the vault apart

The vault is isolated by network rules and by locks. It is still a bucket in a cloud region, and the allowed network reaches it over the network.

The allowed network is inside the allow-list. What did it get from the vault in our tests?

Show the answer

A control from the lab VSI inside the allowed VPC got HTTP 200 (V7, 6 October 2026). That is why this is logical isolation: the vault is reachable, by the networks on its list, and refuses the rest.

Whose account holds the vault's bucket?

Show the answer

Our notes record the answer of 12 October 2026: the vault is in an IBM-managed account. Consistent with that, the bucket was not in our COS instance's bucket list and the configuration API gave the owner 403 (V7, section 4.3).

Our account listed vaults fine on 25 September 2026 but every create failed. What did that show?

Show the answer

Six creates went to failed in 17 to 40 seconds with no error text. A create on 5 October 2026 went to active in about 30 seconds. IBM did not confirm the cause; the most likely one is that the account was not yet on the list. An account on Basic support cannot open the onboarding case; the product team allow-lists it when asked (V1).

Step 4 · Spot the fault

A draft for the auditor

Northgate's draft says: "The vault is a physically air-gapped, production-ready service, and the owner can show who touched it."

Which part is wrong because of what this unit showed?

Show the answer

The vault is logically isolated and reachable by its allowed network. IBM's overview says the Beta has no SLA, no support and is not for production use.

The last claim: the owner can show who touched the vault. What did our test find?

Show the answer

On 6 October 2026 the configuration calls returned HTTP 403 for the account owner. Of 4,346 events in the us-south logs instance, none came from Cloud Object Storage, and a scan of the eu-de instance found no vault event. IBM's overview calls audit logging "fully managed". In our lab an owner could not show an auditor who touched the vault.

Watch for this. Two other Beta findings from our lab: a vault delete was accepted and then stayed in deleting for at least six days, and a failed create left two keys behind on the COS instance. Both are in our feedback record.

Step 5 · Check yourself

Three questions

Which phrase is right for this vault?

Show the answer

The allowed network reaches the vault and got HTTP 200. Everyone else was refused.

Which list of Beta terms is right?

Show the answer

IBM's overview: no warranties, SLA or support, and not for production use.

Is this Beta the Deployable Architecture "COS Cyber Vault for Storage Defender"?

Show the answer

The Beta is a managed COS feature. The Deployable Architecture is a separate product that was never deployed in our lab. Unit 1.4 sets them side by side.
