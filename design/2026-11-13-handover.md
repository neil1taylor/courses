## The process

The README's "Making a video course" section is the owner's view; the `course-video` skill is the
agent's. Load the skill before writing any script. In short:

1. Outline, approved per module on the review page.
2. One sample module (with a diagram, a screenshot and a question), approved, then voiced.
3. The rest in batches of about five, approved before any audio.

Tools, all run from `~/Projects/courses` with `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3.14` (plain `python3` has
no pytest):

- `tools/review.py backup` serves the review page at http://127.0.0.1:8780/ (8770 is another app).
- `tools/review.py backup --export` writes the outline and narration text files to `review/backup/`.
- `tools/review.py backup --import <video folder of a cohesity worktree>` reads them back into
  `outline.json` and the scripts, adds `>` lines as comments, and runs the checks. Then commit
  through a PR in cohesity and run `--export` again.
- `--comments` lists open comments; `--close <id>` closes them.
- Tests: `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3.14 -m pytest tests -q` (202 pass) and
  `node --test tools/video/player/test/` (27 pass).
